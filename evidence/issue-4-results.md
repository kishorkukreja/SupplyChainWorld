# Issue #4 implementation and verification evidence

Implemented `src/supplychainworld/verification.py` independently of solver code. Structural validation uses issue #3 public contracts; all business arithmetic is independently calculated. The checker accepts mappings and validated models, rejects malformed inputs with a structured failure, and reports acceptance, individual checks, cost, failure reasons, and optional objective gap.

Actual validation: `PYTHONPATH=src python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_verification.py -v` completed successfully: 5 test methods. Full output is in `issue-4-test-results.txt`. The table test checks all ten expected submissions, every fixture's known wrong submissions, and listed valid alternatives. Additional checks cover invalid units, duplicate allocations, fractional quantities, doubled claimed fees, half-up supplier-subtotal rounding, valid tied awards, ID relabeling, zero spend/concentration, fake exceptions, malformed worlds and empty evidence.

Confirmed semantics: usable stock and timely committed inbound reduce demand; positive awards enforce qualification, quote dates, arrival deadline, capacity, MOQ and multiples; excess is explicit; rounded supplier landed subtotals include fixed charges once; their sum drives budget and concentration. Feasible suboptimal awards remain accepted and quality is reported separately. The final business playbook supersedes the initial provisional plan's line-rounding proposal: rounding is per supplier subtotal, not per line.

Limitations: infeasibility acceptance currently proves item-capacity shortfalls only (including eligible multiples and MOQ). It conservatively rejects other infeasibility diagnoses rather than trusting a solver status or timeout. Clarification verifies that the named commercial field is truly missing; environment/task dispatch must enforce the task's allowed clarification fields and record-and-escalate policy. Natural-language evidence is checked for supported diagnosis and the numerical proof is independently generated; this is not unrestricted semantic entailment checking. Objective gap requires caller-supplied verified optimal reference cost; it is undefined for positive actual cost against zero reference. No practitioner or domain signoff is claimed; all fixture reviews remain pending.
