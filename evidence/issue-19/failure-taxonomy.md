Every failure and anomaly in the run, classified from inspected records (private raw answers and transcripts, the checker output, the prompt text). Classes: task (the world or its rules), grader (checker or verifier), harness (prompt, tool definition, adapter, runner), model. Nothing was tuned after the freeze, so none of these was fixed or rerun here. "Corrective" lists what to change before another run.
