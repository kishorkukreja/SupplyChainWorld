## Scenarios and declared policies
Scenarios (five): sudden 5d (standard part); warned and delayed 10d (custom); warned and no delay 10d (custom, same level and cards as its twin); multi-tier with cancellation 10d (standard); **new** slow-expedite-lane 10d (standard) where alternate is clearly cheaper than expedite on net cost.
Policies (scripted, read only the visible view, unchanged from v0.2): maintain; expedite on alert; alternate on alert; combo on alert; staged revisit.

## Verification approach
1. Fresh hand-worked ledgers for 25 episodes. They are regenerated by `scripts/generate_hand_ledgers_v03.py`, committed so the process is reproducible. The script imports no project code; its inputs are the scenario numbers and a table of the commitments each declared policy makes, worked out by hand from the written rules. Two scenarios (sudden 5d, slow lane) were also worked fully by hand on paper before the script ran; the script output matched.
2. Independent verifier `dynamic_verifier_v3` imports no simulator code (AST test) and re-derives each ledger from commands.
3. Compare simulator, verifier and hand ledgers for all 25 episodes; investigate any mismatch.
4. Adversarial tests: duplicate commitment, over-budget combination, premature receipt and recovery, wrong penalty, ignored penalty cap, wrong part class, wrong premium on either card, wrong cancellation lag, wrong tier weights, tampering, forged scorecard, leaked early signal (outcome wording, numeric hint, wrong level).
5. Replay every episode on a fresh world; scan every episode's observations for private answers and outcome leaks; test that the warned twins are observation-identical until the firm notice.
